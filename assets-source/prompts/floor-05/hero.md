# Floor 05 — Projeto completo (single hero image)

Copy the entire prompt below as-is into your image generation tool.

```
Photorealistic architectural interior photograph, wide-angle editorial
real-estate magazine style, the most impressive and aspirational shot in
the series. A fully finished, beautifully styled compact studio
apartment, shot with a full-frame DSLR, 20mm wide-angle lens, camera
positioned slightly elevated (~1.7m) near a back corner of the room,
looking across the whole space on a gentle diagonal so multiple zones are
visible at once: the sleeping/lounge wall, a sliver of a small kitchenette
or dining nook at the edge of frame, and open floor space in between.
Natural soft daylight flooding in from a large out-of-frame window,
warm color temperature (~4500K), soft realistic shadows, no HDR look,
no harsh highlights.

Room architecture: light gray-beige painted walls, warm oak engineered
wood flooring throughout, simple modern white baseboards, plain ceiling
with two small recessed downlights, off (daylight only).

Hero furniture system (the main subject, fully in frame, generously lit):
on the back wall, two wall-mounted cabinets in light oak wood veneer with
brushed-aluminum trim, matching the same product family used throughout
this series — a narrower left cabinet (~100cm) shown CLOSED, flush and
seamless, and a wider right cabinet (~140cm) shown OPEN, folded down into
a Murphy-style wall bed: white powder-coated tubular steel leg frame at
the foot end, small orange rubber pads on the folding hinge rail, a
neatly made single mattress with plain white fitted sheet, one white
pillow, a folded neutral-linen throw at the foot.

Supporting furniture and decor (this exact set, nothing extra): a low
two-seat charcoal-gray fabric sofa angled near the foot of the bed
without touching it, a round light-oak coffee table in front of the sofa
with one ceramic bowl, a jute area rug under the seating area, one tall
potted olive tree in a terracotta pot near the window side, one small
open shelving unit in light oak on a side wall with a few folded towels
and books styled neatly, one framed minimal line-art print on the wall
above the sofa. At the edge of frame, partially visible: a slim light-oak
kitchenette counter with one pendant light above it, suggesting the rest
of the apartment continues beyond the shot.

Overall mood: the culmination shot — sophisticated, warm, spacious-
feeling despite being compact, aspirational, believable, exactly like the
cover image of a real furniture brand's editorial lookbook. Not a render,
not CGI-looking, not oversaturated. Sharp focus throughout, natural depth
of field (f/8 equivalent). Aspect ratio 3:2, landscape, with comfortable
margin around the main subject for later cropping.

No people. No text or logos anywhere in the image. No visible brand names.
```

## Mechanism reference

Same hardware family as the rest of the series: wood panel hinged at
wall/top, white metal folding leg frame visible when open, orange rubber
pads on the hinge rail, mattress resting flat and level, no floating
parts, no impossible geometry.
